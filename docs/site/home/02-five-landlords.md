## You don't own your online presence today. You rent it — from five landlords.

A website builder for the site. WordPress and a dozen plugins for the blog. Google or Microsoft for the mail. An analytics vendor watching your readers. A SaaS dashboard tying it together — each its own bill, its own login, its own cloud you don't control. VayuPress is the opposite bet: your website, your blog, your mail, your analytics and your admin in one binary you run yourself — nothing phones home, nothing is rented, nothing can be taken away.

### Own your content

A real website and a Ghost-class blog — block editor, whole-site themes, media, members, SEO — with every word in your own SQLite file. No SaaS, no export wall.

### Own your communication

A native SMTP/IMAP/POP3 mail server with DKIM and automatic PGP is built into the binary — send and receive from your own domain, read it in the official app, no third-party relay.

### Own your privacy

End-to-end PGP, encrypted keys at rest, and cookieless analytics with zero trackers and no PII on your readers. The only outbound calls are the ones you configure.
